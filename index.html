Under the hood

Thin launcher. Proven engines.

Rollout does not patch games. It drives open projects that already do the hard work.

Verified downloads

Upstream scripts are pinned to an exact commit and checked with SHA-256. Launcher updates are signed with Ed25519.

Stays out of your system

Rollout lives in ~/.local/share/rollout. Installing needs no root and changes no system packages on SteamOS or Bazzite.

Honest about limits

Zwift has no direct Bluetooth in its container, and neither game is officially supported on Linux. Rollout says so instead of hiding it.

FAQ

Questions riders ask.

Does Zwift run on Steam Deck and Bazzite?

On Bazzite it does: Zwift runs in a container based on netbrain/zwift, which Rollout installs and starts. Rollout is tested on a ROG Ally with Bazzite; the Steam Deck is not yet verified. SteamOS usually needs Podman or Docker first. Sensors connect through Zwift Companion on your phone or Direct Connect, not directly over Bluetooth.

Does MyWhoosh run on SteamOS and Linux with Bluetooth?

Yes. MyWhoosh runs with GE-Proton and talks to your trainer and heart-rate strap over Bluetooth directly. You pair them in the game, like on Windows.

Does Zwift work on the ROG Ally?

Yes, on Bazzite. Install Rollout in Desktop Mode, then start Zwift from Rollout or from Steam in Gaming Mode. Your controller steers the game.

Do I need a terminal?

No. Download Rollout.desktop, double-click it in Desktop Mode and confirm once. Rollout then appears in your app menu.

Can I use a controller?

Yes. Rollout works with gamepad, touch and keyboard. In Gaming Mode your controller also steers Zwift.

How do updates work?

Rollout checks for new versions in the background and installs them automatically if you want. Launcher updates are signed, and a broken update rolls back on its own.

Is Rollout official? Can my account get banned?

No. Rollout is an independent project, not affiliated with Zwift, Inc., MyWhoosh or Valve. Their terms restrict unapproved third-party tools, and they can suspend accounts at their discretion. Read the terms and decide for yourself.

Which devices and systems are supported?

Rollout is in beta. It is tested on a ROG Ally with Bazzite. SteamOS on the Steam Deck is the second target but not yet verified. Other Linux distributions are at your own risk.
